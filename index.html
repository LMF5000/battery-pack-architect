About Battery Pack Architect

Battery Pack Architect is a free, open-source 3D tool for designing custom lithium battery packs. Visualize Series/Parallel configurations (18650, 21700, LFP, Na-ion) in real-time.

  • 3D Visualization: Auto-generates spot-welding strips & hex layouts.
  • Physics Engine: Simulates Voltage Sag, Joule Heating, and Efficiency losses.
  • Dynamic Runtime: Estimates runtime based on Constant Power/Current loads and SoC.
  • Chemistry Aware: Accurate curves for Li-ion, LiFePO4 (LFP), and LTO.
